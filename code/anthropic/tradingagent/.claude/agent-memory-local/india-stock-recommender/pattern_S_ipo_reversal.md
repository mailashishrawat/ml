## Pattern S — IPO/Post-Listing Reversal

Discovered and formalized on 2026-05-26 after analyzing user's successful ATHER (ATHERENERG) trade.

**Why:** User bought ATHER on May 22 at Rs 883.8, sold May 26 at Rs 981.1 = +11% in 4 days on Rs 1 lakh.
Pipeline did NOT recommend it (confidence ~65%). The miss prompted creation of this pattern.

**The Pattern:**
After a recently listed stock (6-24 months post-IPO) corrects 8-20% from its listing-era high in 6-10 sessions with DECLINING volume on red days:
- Entry: RSI reaches 38-50 (exhaustion zone, not extreme oversold)
- Trigger: Price reclaims 5-day MA on the first recovery day
- Volume: Recovery day volume >= 1.0x 20-day average (minimum); >= 1.5x = strong signal

**Required Conditions:**
1. Listed 6-24 months ago and ran UP meaningfully from IPO price first (>20%)
2. Correction from "listing-era high": 8-20% in 6-10 sessions
3. Volume on DOWN days: lower-than-average (sellers exhausted) — NOT high vol (that's institutional selling)
4. RSI 38-50 at entry point
5. 5-day MA reclaimed on first recovery day
6. Strong narrative: EV, food delivery, new economy, defense tech, solar
7. No company-specific negative news

**Confidence Scoring (Technical):**
Base 72 (overrides the 75% new-listing cap when full criteria met)
+5 for EV/Green macro tailwind
+5 for duopoly peer running (Pattern A overlay)
+5 for volume >= 1.2x avg on recovery
+5 for RSI clearly 42-50 (not borderline 35-38)
+5 for revenue growth or loss improvement in recent quarter
Technical maximum: 72 + 25 = 97. Realistic: 80-87

## Pattern S — Sub-Pattern: Loss-to-Profit Inflection (LPI)

**Added 2026-05-26** — the critical missing element the user identified after the ATHER trade.

**What LPI Is:**
The ATHER trade worked not just because of the chart reversal but because of a fundamental pre-buying thesis:
ATHER has been consistently reducing its losses for 4+ consecutive quarters and is expected to turn profitable
in Q1 FY27. Institutions front-run the "first profit quarter" by 1-2 quarters, creating sustained accumulation
pressure that SUPPORTS and EXTENDS the chart reversal beyond a simple technical bounce.

**LPI Signal Definition:**
- 4+ consecutive quarters of improving financials: losses narrowing OR revenue accelerating even if still loss-making
- The key signal: EBITDA or PAT trending from deep negative toward zero (the "path to first profit")
- Expected first profitable quarter is 1-3 quarters away
- Institutions begin accumulating 1-2 quarters before that event

**LPI Confidence Boost: +8 (single highest-value boost in Pattern S framework)**

- Makes Pattern S candidate a STRONG BUY (not just a WATCH) when combined with chart reversal
- Full combination: technical Pattern S + LPI sub-pattern = confidence range 82-90

**LPI Disqualifiers (do NOT apply the +8 boost):**
- Revenue also declining (improvement only via cost-cuts = unsustainable; LPI requires revenue growth too)
- Most recent quarter loss WIDENED vs 2 quarters ago (breaks the improvement streak)
- Debt rising rapidly (signals improvement is not real)

**Holding Window Implication:**
Technical Pattern S alone = T+4 to T+8 (momentum bounce)
Pattern S + LPI = T+10 to T+30 (institutional accumulation thesis plays out over weeks)
