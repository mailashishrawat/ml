## Pattern Library

### Pattern A — Duopoly / Sector Rotation Lag
When one stock in a known pair rallies, the peer typically catches up within 2-3 sessions.
Accuracy: Validated strongly on May 8 run (IRCON vs RVNL, INOXWIND vs Suzlon).
Extension: Works for T&D sector rotation (BHEL ran → KEC/CGPOWER catch up).

### Pattern C — RSI Recovery
Stocks recovering from below RSI 30 to RSI 42-52 = ideal entry zone.
Sweet spot: RSI 42-50 at entry. Never enter above RSI 65 on a 2-day trade.
Validated on May 8 (all 5 picks had RSI 44-54 at entry).

### Pattern D — Institutional Strength Dip
Stocks above RSI 55 for 30+ days, temporary dip to RSI 45-52, DII support present.
High confidence (70-75%) when FII selling is identified as rotation (not fundamentals).

### Pattern G — Trade Truce / Macro Catalyst Beneficiary (NEW, May 11)
When a major global macro positive occurs (trade truce, rate cut, ceasefire), identify
which Indian mid-caps are DIRECT beneficiaries vs indirect. IT services and specialty
chemicals are the most direct Indian beneficiaries of US-China trade stability.

### Pattern H — Monday Post-Macro-Event Gap-Up Continuation
First Monday after a major weekend macro positive (US-China truce etc.) tends to see
mid-cap continuation for 2 full sessions. Strategy: buy at Monday open, exit by T+2.
RSI entry zone for this pattern: 42-52 (any higher and you're chasing).
