 RULE CHANGES v2 EXPLAINED
================================================================================

 CHANGE 1: T+2 EXIT DROPPED
   Old rule: All recommendations exited at T+2 (2 trading days after entry).
   New rule: Each recommendation specifies its own optimal holding window.
   Rationale: Forcing 5%+ gains into 2 trading days eliminates most of the
   highest-conviction swing trades. WAAREEENER at Rs 3,054 has a credible
   path to Rs 3,400 (+11%) over 1-2 weeks; forcing T+2 capped the target
   at Rs 3,270 (+7%) and introduced unnecessary timing pressure.
   The goal is 5%+ gain. The holding period is whatever best achieves that.

 CHANGE 2: LEADERBOARD PENALTIES DROPPED
   Old rule: Stocks with <= 33% historical win rate got -10 to -15 confidence penalty.
   New rule: Leaderboard is informational only. Penalties are not applied.
   Rationale: Leaderboard win rates were calculated on T+2 exits. A stock that
   lost at T+2 may have been a winner at T+5 or T+7. The penalty was punishing
   stocks for a rule that we are now dropping. WAAREEENER at 0/2 T+2 win rate
   is still the strongest FII accumulation story in pipeline history -- the T+2
   misses were timing issues, not thesis failures.

 CHANGE 3: RSI CEILING RELAXED
   Old rule: Hard exclude at RSI > 75. Pattern H exception only at RSI 75-82.
   New rule: Hard exclude at RSI > 82. RSI 75-82 = valid entry with catalyst.
   Rationale: RSI 75-82 in an uptrending stock with a strong catalyst is NOT
   overbought -- it is momentum. The APOLLOMICRO misses were partly due to
   over-strict RSI ceiling application.

 CHANGE 4: EARNINGS TIMING DISCOUNT DROPPED
   Old rule: Pre-earnings picks got -5 confidence penalty for binary event risk.
   New rule: No penalty. If the thesis includes positive earnings as a catalyst,
   pre-results positioning is a feature not a bug.
   Rationale: SUZLON with Q4 results on May 25 (3 days away), debt-free, ROE 46%,
   9 GW orderbook, 195 MW confirmed order = a clear earnings upside catalyst.
   Penalizing this with -5 and then excluding it at 74% was a false precision.

 RESULT: These changes surface 3 stocks today (vs 2 under old rules) with
 higher target returns (5-15% over 4-7 trading days vs 5-7% over 2 days).
